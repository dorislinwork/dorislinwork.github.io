Taiwanese sumer vibe

Personal work | 2025

Summer has arrived in Taiwan! One of the best things about it is enjoying a bowl of shaved ice topped with chewy tangyuan, taro balls, and Taiwan’s famously sweet mangoes.

Dipping tomatoes in soy sauce is a unique eating tradition you’ll find at old-school ice shops in southern Taiwan.

Don’t leave Taiwan without trying it!!✨
