Rudolph! | Interactive Projection Game

Personal project | 2020

“Rudolph” is an interactive projection table game. The goal of the game is to collect as much garbage as possible in 100 seconds. There are up and downhills and rivers in the game, also the traps of the spike, which might let players undergo the short-lived death.

Players have three actual item boards respectively used as three different functions.

For example, Rudolph can jump with the “pudding” board, the “conveyor belt” makes Rudolph speed up, and the “fan” helps to move further.

There are also many different terrains in the game. Players need to put each item board in the proper places and have the flexibility to use them under time pressure to get points, which gives players excitement and pleasure.
